# Changelog

## 0.1.3
First release.
- Bulk Storage Bin (Hammer > Storage): holds one kind of stone, ore, scrap, bone, resin or fuel at a time, in a big 8 x 4 window. The bin fills up with pieces of the material you put in, a sign shows what it holds, and the pile is solid, so you cannot stand inside it.
- Woodpile Rack (Hammer > Storage): the same idea for wood and logs, stacked in the rack's V. The hover text shows the wood it holds.
- Small Cabinet (4 x 2 slots) and Large Cabinet (4 x 4 slots): the window matches the shelves, and each shelf shows the item in its slot. Stacks are normal vanilla stacks, a shelf shows one of them. A slot takes whatever a vanilla item stand can show.
- Cabinets have doors that open with the window and close with it. They snap to each other on the sides, top, bottom and back, and the front stays free.
- Config: extra items for the bin, extra and blocked items for the cabinets (synced from the server), fill display, no-build space above the bins.
- Fix: the Large Cabinet window showed no slots the first time it was opened (vanilla keeps no cells for a 4 x 4 grid until another size has been shown).
- E works on the stones, ore or logs inside a bin or rack too, not only on the frame.
- Needs Balrond Constructions (the cabinet uses some of its trim pieces, used with Balrond's permission).
